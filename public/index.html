A closer look, in three steps.

1

Bring the claim

Paste text, upload an article image, or record a voice clip. Each input is checked separately. Review voice transcripts before checking.

2

Examine the evidence

Verity compares checkable claims with retrieved sources. A headline alone cannot establish a verdict.

3

Read the full picture

True and False reflect the available evidence. Unverified means there is not enough evidence. Articles with different findings are checked claim by claim. The evidence coverage percentage counts claims with a clear verdict and a citation; it is not an accuracy probability.
